Understanding anxiety

Anxiety is your body's natural alarm system — it becomes a concern when the alarm won't turn off.

Some anxiety is normal and even helpful — it keeps you alert before exams, careful near danger. It becomes a concern when it's excessive, persistent, or starts interfering with daily life.

Meet Calm — your anxiety companion

Calm is an AI-powered chatbot trained to help you understand anxiety, learn coping techniques, and feel less alone. It remembers your conversation so you can pick up right where you left off.

Ask anything — from "what is anxiety?" to "I'm feeling overwhelmed right now."
